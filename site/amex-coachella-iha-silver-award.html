AmEx + Coachella Landing Page — IHA Silver Award

I was the developer on the AmEx + Coachella landing page, which won a Silver Award for Digital Marketing, B2C Websites at the 2026 IHA Creativity Awards.

The work was part of a series of event-led member benefit experiences for American Express, built as time-bound landing pages around live events.

The work

The AmEx + Coachella landing page was one of a series of event-led member benefit experiences built for American Express. I built the page as a time-bound campaign experience that helped members understand and access benefits around the event.

The broader body of work included landing page experiences for events such as NBA All Star Weekend, Formula 1 races, Austin City Limits, and Stagecoach Music Festival.

Recognition

The 2026 IHA Creativity Awards listing names the Amex® + Coachella Cardmember Experience Landing Page under Websites, B2C, within Digital Marketing. The award recognizes the landing page experience created for the campaign.
